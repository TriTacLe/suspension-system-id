      Work out the frequencies

      W appears here

      W is roughly the frequency the suspension wants to oscillate at, and it sets every sine run below. W = 6.28 / T.

      DonePointRad/sSave as

      Amplitude 1 every time. Let each run go at least ten full cycles, since the first few are settling and get thrown away.

      If you run short of time

      The two marked runs come first. The peak near W is your best evidence of damping, and the far point at 6W is what keeps the roll-off honest. Measured only between 2W and 4W the slope reads near -60 dB per decade and the report looks like the model failed when it did not.

      Logging is fixed at 100 ms, so anything above about 15 rad/s has too few samples per cycle to fit. If a frequency lands above 15, enter 15 and say in the report that the sampler set the ceiling.

      4

      Watch while they run

      Note roughly how big the output swing is at each frequency and how far behind the input it lags. The swing should grow towards W and shrink after. If it looks biggest between two of your frequencies, run one extra in that gap: that peak is the strongest evidence you will get all day and an extra log costs three minutes.

      The lag should grow steadily, about a quarter cycle behind at W and approaching half a cycle far above it.
